title: Adult Medication Dosage Protocols
topic: clinical
allowed_roles: [clinical]
---
Paracetamol (acetaminophen): the standard adult dose is 500 mg to 1 g every 4 to 6 hours. The maximum is 4 g in 24 hours. For patients under 50 kg or with liver impairment the maximum is 3 g in 24 hours.

Amoxicillin: the standard adult oral dose for community-acquired infection is 500 mg every 8 hours for 5 to 7 days. Severe infection may use 1 g every 8 hours under physician order.

Unfractionated heparin: the initial IV bolus is 80 units per kg, followed by an infusion of 18 units per kg per hour. Adjust the infusion according to the aPTT nomogram every 6 hours.

Any dose above the listed maximums requires a second clinician sign-off recorded in the medication chart.
