topic: general

Full-time staff receive 25 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Leave requests should be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the staff portal.

Sick leave requires a medical certificate after 3 consecutive days of absence. Unused annual leave may carry over up to 5 days into the next year.
