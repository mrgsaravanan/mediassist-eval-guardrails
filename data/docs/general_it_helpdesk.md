title: IT Helpdesk and Password Policy
topic: general
allowed_roles: [clinical, billing, hr, general]
---
The IT helpdesk is available 24 hours a day at extension 4000 or through the staff portal. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are rotated every 180 days.

Staff must never share credentials. Lost badges or suspected account compromise must be reported to the helpdesk immediately. Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access.
