Salary bands for 2026: Registered Nurse band N3 ranges from 68,000 to 82,000 USD. Senior Nurse band N4 ranges from 80,000 to 98,000 USD. Department Manager band M2 ranges from 105,000 to 135,000 USD.

Annual merit increases are capped at 4 percent unless the CHRO grants an exception. Compensation details are confidential to the HR team and executive leadership.
