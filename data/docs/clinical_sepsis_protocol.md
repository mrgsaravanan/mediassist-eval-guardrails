title: Sepsis Recognition and Response Protocol
topic: clinical
allowed_roles: [clinical]
---
Screen every adult inpatient with the qSOFA score: respiratory rate of 22 or more, altered mentation, and systolic blood pressure of 100 mmHg or less. A score of 2 or more triggers the sepsis pathway.

The sepsis bundle must be completed within 1 hour: measure lactate, take blood cultures before antibiotics, give broad-spectrum antibiotics, and start 30 mL per kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate of 4 mmol/L or more.

Escalate to the critical care outreach team if lactate remains above 2 mmol/L after the first fluid bolus.
