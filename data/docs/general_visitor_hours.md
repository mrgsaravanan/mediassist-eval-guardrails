topic: general
allowed_roles: [clinical, billing, hr, general]

General visiting hours are 10:00 to 20:00 daily. The intensive care unit allows visits from 14:00 to 16:00 and 18:00 to 19:30, limited to two visitors at a time.

All visitors must sign in at the front desk and wear a visitor badge. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. Visitors with cold or flu symptoms are asked to postpone their visit.
