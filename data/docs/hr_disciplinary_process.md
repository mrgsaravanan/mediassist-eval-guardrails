The disciplinary process has three stages: verbal warning, written warning, and final written warning. Termination requires HR director approval and documented evidence.

Investigations of misconduct must begin within 5 business days of a complaint and be concluded within 30 days. The employee has the right to be accompanied to any disciplinary hearing.
