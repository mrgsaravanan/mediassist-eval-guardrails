title: Insurance Claims Processing Policy
topic: billing
allowed_roles: [billing]
---
Insurance claims must be submitted within 90 days of the date of service. Claims submitted after 90 days are written off unless a documented payer error exists.

Denied claims are appealed within 30 days using form CL-7. The billing team tracks the denial reason code and reports the monthly denial rate to finance.

Prior authorization is required for MRI, CT, and elective surgical procedures. The authorization number must appear on the claim.
