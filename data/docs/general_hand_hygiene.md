title: Hand Hygiene Policy
topic: general
allowed_roles: [clinical, billing, hr, general]
---
All staff must perform hand hygiene before and after patient contact, after contact with patient surroundings, and after removing gloves. Alcohol-based hand rub is preferred unless hands are visibly soiled.

When hands are visibly soiled, wash with soap and water for at least 20 seconds. Compliance is audited monthly on every ward, and the target compliance rate is 95 percent.
